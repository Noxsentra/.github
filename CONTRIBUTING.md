# Contributing to Noxsentra

Thank you for your interest in Noxsentra, a Magnexis research initiative focused on cybersecurity, AI security, and emerging threat intelligence.

## Ways to contribute

- Improve defensive tooling, documentation, and tests.
- Propose research questions and evidence-backed improvements.
- Correct inaccurate citations or vulnerability metadata.
- Share publicly redistributable security resources with clear source attribution.
- Help make security interfaces accessible and easy to understand.

## Before opening a pull request

1. Open a non-sensitive issue to discuss substantial scope changes when a suitable issue tracker exists.
2. Keep contributions focused, technically reproducible, and appropriately documented.
3. Identify relevant sources, dependencies, and licenses. Do not submit data or models that you lack the rights to redistribute.
4. Add tests or validation steps when applicable.
5. Clearly label speculative or unverified statements.

## Research integrity

- Do not represent an unconfirmed issue as an exploited or verified vulnerability.
- No unauthorized scanning, exploitation, or disclosure of confidential data.
- Follow each affected vendor's disclosure rules and coordinate privately when necessary.
- Do not upload credentials, personal information, malware samples, or operational exploit payloads into public repositories.

**Security reports:** Follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Never disclose exploitable vulnerabilities in public issue threads.

Noxsentra is an early-stage research initiative. Participation does not imply employment, compensation, a commercial partnership, or endorsement.
