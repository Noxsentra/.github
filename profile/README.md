## About Noxsentra

**Noxsentra** is the security research and threat-intelligence initiative of **Magnexis**. Our goal is to investigate emerging digital risks, develop defensive technologies, and translate rigorous technical research into useful intelligence for developers, researchers, and organizations.

We are building a focused research practice across cyber threat intelligence, AI system security, vulnerability research, and secure engineering. Our work is intended to prioritize **verifiable evidence, clear technical documentation, and coordinated vulnerability disclosure**.

> **Organization status:** Noxsentra is an early-stage Magnexis research initiative. This GitHub presence does not imply a separately incorporated subsidiary, existing commercial security certifications, or partnerships with named technology companies.

## Focus areas

| Research area | Scope |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Emerging Threat Intelligence** | Tracking advisories, correlating trusted vulnerability sources, and producing defensible assessments of emerging threats. |
| **AI & Model Security** | Studying AI application risks, model and dataset supply chains, prompt-injection defenses, agent/tool boundaries, and security evaluations. |
| **Vulnerability Research** | Investigating security weaknesses **only with authorization**, documenting reproducible evidence, and supporting coordinated disclosure. |
| **Defensive Engineering** | Building security tooling, safer developer workflows, and practical mitigations for identified risks. |
| **Applied AI & Software Research** | Exploring datasets, model evaluation and training workflows, secure software design, and usable interfaces for security research. |

## How we approach research

```text
Trusted sources and permitted research
                |
                v
     Triage and source validation
                |
                v
     Technical analysis and review
                |
                v
     Evidence-backed internal record
                |
                v
   Vendor coordination when relevant
                |
                v
  Defensive guidance / public research
```

We distinguish **reported**, **suspected**, and **verified** findings; cite original sources; and do not treat automated or AI-generated assessments as confirmed vulnerabilities without appropriate validation.

## Initiatives in development

- **Emerging Threat Watch (ETW)** — A proposed research and intelligence system for source ingestion, normalization, correlation, and analyst-reviewed threat records.
- **AI Supply Chain Research** — Investigation of defensive risks affecting model repositories, datasets, libraries, and AI agent integrations.
- **Security Research Publications** — A public home for technical write-ups, reproducible methodology, and vetted mitigation guidance.

These are areas of active planning and development, **not claims that all systems are already deployed or that findings have been independently validated**.

## Responsible disclosure

We support good-faith, authorized security research and responsible disclosure.

- **Do not** test systems without permission or outside applicable program rules.
- **Do not** post unpatched exploit details, sensitive data, or credentials in public issues.
- Give affected maintainers a reasonable opportunity to investigate and remediate before public disclosure.
- Prefer factual, reproducible, minimally invasive evidence and clear mitigation guidance.

For vulnerabilities **in Noxsentra-managed code**, consult the relevant repository's `SECURITY.md` and any enabled private vulnerability reporting channel. For vulnerabilities in **third-party systems**, follow the affected vendor's official security disclosure policy.

## Collaboration

We welcome constructive collaboration on **defensive security research, open-source tooling, AI security, vulnerability intelligence, and secure engineering**.

For nonsensitive proposals, use an appropriate public repository's discussion or issue channel where one is available. Do not send confidential vulnerability details through public issues.
