# Noxsentra Community Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want collaboration to be rigorous, inclusive, and safe for researchers, defenders, maintainers, and people affected by security issues. Participants are expected to criticize ideas and evidence constructively, not attack individuals.

## Expected behavior

- Treat contributors and affected parties respectfully, including when evaluating conflicting security claims.
- Support technical assertions with sources, clear scope, and reproducible evidence where possible.
- Respect authorization boundaries, privacy, embargoes, and coordinated disclosure requests.
- Welcome correction, differing interpretations, and good-faith feedback.
- Declare relevant conflicts of interest when making research recommendations.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, doxxing, unwanted sexual attention, intimidation, deliberately misleading security claims, unauthorized access, or sharing credentials, personal records, or undisclosed vulnerabilities publicly.

## Moderation and reporting

Maintainers may remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation when conduct threatens the community or affected systems. For non-sensitive conduct concerns, contact a repository maintainer through the available GitHub channels. Do not include sensitive personal data or security exploits in public issues. For security vulnerabilities, use [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy applies to Noxsentra-operated repositories and official project discussion spaces. It does not replace GitHub's platform policies or grant authorization for security testing.
